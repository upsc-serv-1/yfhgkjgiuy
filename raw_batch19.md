- New provisions — **Retributive, victim-centric, tech-enabled, simplified**.

  - 1. S.110 (org crime).
  - 2. S.111 (terrorism).

  - 4. **Mob lynching — 7 years.**
  - 5. Police accountability.

  - 1. Zero FIR.
  - 2. Mandatory videography.
  - 3. Timelines — chargesheet (90 days) & judgement (30 days).
  - 4. Compulsory forensics.
  - 5. Community service.
- **IEA changes**: Admissibility of **electronic/digital records**.
- **Issues**:
  - 1. Hate speech removed.
  - 2. Detention time increased.
  - 3. Low bail focus.
  - 4. Vague terms.
:::

:::conclusion
**MoHA**: "The spirit of the 3 new laws is not to punish but to give justice to every citizen & prevent further crimes."
