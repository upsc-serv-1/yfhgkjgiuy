HOW TO USE THIS BOOK

Read cards, not chapters

This book is a deck of 551 answer cards. Each card is self-contained. You can open any page and revise for five useful minutes.

How one card is built

The sample below is a real card from Chapter 1. The labels explain each block. Every card in the book follows the same grammar.
CARD TITLE + ID

The title names the examinable idea. The mono ID (like GS1-HIST-0001) is a stable handle — search it with Ctrl+F.

QUOTE

A verified quotable line with its attribution. Gold bar marks it. One such line per answer earns marks safely.

INTRODUCTION

A two-to-three line opening you can lift straight into an answer.

EXAMPLES · DATA · DETAIL

Ready evidence: schemes, cases, judgments, figures. Data blocks sit on a teal-ruled panel; detail blocks carry a gold label.

CONCLUSION

A closing sentence with a forward look — the safest way to end an answer.

Three ways to reach any card

Every page carries a breadcrumb at the top: paper · subject · section. Click it to jump back. Or use one of these:

1 · Part openers list each paper's chapters and heaviest subtopics 2 · The master index lists every card, GS-wise then subject-wise 3 · Search the card ID — every ID is unique across the book

Reading the visuals

Part openers carry three small visuals. The donut shows each subject's share of the cards. The tier rows rank the heaviest syllabus sections. The heatmap shows how many cards sit on each subtopic — darker means heavier.

Three reading routes

Pick one route and finish it before the exam. Each route reads the same cards — only the order and the daily load change.

ROUTE WHO IT SUITS PLAN
30 days · Sprint Mains is close and you have read the syllabus once. Read the heaviest subtopics of every subject first — roughly 180 cards — at 6 cards a day. Revise each day's cards next morning.
60 days · Steady You want full coverage of the two big papers first. Finish GS-2 and GS-4 (265 cards) in the first 25 days. Then read GS-1 and GS-3 in the next 30 days. Keep the last 5 days for the index run.
90 days · Foundation This is your first full Mains cycle. Read the whole book paper by paper, 6 to 7 cards a day. Write one full answer for every ten cards. Use Sundays to re-read the quotes.
One habit. After every ten cards, close the book and write two answer paragraphs from memory. The routes above only work if this habit stays.
