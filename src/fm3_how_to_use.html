HOW TO USE THIS BOOK

Read cards, not chapters

This book is a deck of 551 answer cards. Each card is self-contained. You can open any page and revise for five useful minutes.

How one card is built

Every card sits in its own white rounded box, with its paper's colour on the top edge. Inside, each part of the answer carries a clear label:

Card title
GS1-HIST-0001
Quote

A verified quotable line with its attribution, on a soft gold panel. One such line per answer earns marks safely.

Introduction

A two-to-three line opening you can lift straight into an answer.

Examples

Ready evidence: schemes, cases, judgments, committees, and reports.

Data

Safe figures and dates on a teal-ruled mono panel.

Conclusion

A closing sentence with a forward look — the safest way to end an answer.

Cards may also carry detail panels (gold label, extra depth) and images where a diagram helps. The mono ID on every card is a stable handle — search it with Ctrl+F.

How to reach any card

1 · Subject-wise index. The pages right after this one list all 551 card titles, grouped paper by paper, then subject, section, and subtopic. Click any title to jump straight to its card.
2 · The INDEX button. On screen, a round INDEX button stays at the bottom-right corner of every page. One click brings you back to the index from anywhere in the book.
3 · Breadcrumbs. Every page carries a trail at the top — paper, subject, section. Click any step to jump back, or search a card ID with Ctrl+F.

Three reading routes

Pick one route and finish it before the exam. Each route reads the same cards — only the order and the daily load change.

ROUTE WHO IT SUITS PLAN
30 days · Sprint Mains is close and you have read the syllabus once. Read the biggest subtopics of every subject first — roughly 180 cards — at 6 cards a day. Revise each day's cards next morning.
60 days · Steady You want full coverage of the two big papers first. Finish GS-2 and GS-4 (265 cards) in the first 25 days. Then read GS-1 and GS-3 in the next 30 days. Keep the last 5 days for the index run.
90 days · Foundation This is your first full Mains cycle. Read the whole book paper by paper, 6 to 7 cards a day. Write one full answer for every ten cards. Use Sundays to re-read the quotes.
One habit. After every ten cards, close the book and write two answer paragraphs from memory. The routes above only work if this habit stays.
