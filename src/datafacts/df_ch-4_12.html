WATER & ITS CONSERVATION
Water Scarcity
Water Scarcity: As per World Bank, Annual per-capita water availability
< 1700 m³: Water Stressed condition
< 1000 m³: Water Scarcity condition (India =: Around 1500 m³ (CWC))
Global Scenario: Water scarcity affects more than 40% of the global population
<b>India’s Scenario</b>
18% of world population lives in India but it has just 4% of world’s water resources.
India is the second-largest consumer of water, using 20.1 per cent of global water resources.
It ranks 133rd in water availability per capita
Per Capita Use (India): Average annual per capita water availability for the year 2021 was 1,486 cubic meters (Ministry of Jal Shakti)
Projected water demand: Per capita water use is projected to increase from 85 litres per capita per day (lpcd) in 2000 to 125 lpcd by 2025 and 170 lpcd by 2050.
Access: 50% has no access to safe water.
Deaths: Around 2 Lac/year (due to lack of access to safe water)
Economic Burden: Loss of 6% of GDP
Water Pollution
Surface Water Pollution: 80% of surface water is polluted
Pollution Levels: 70% water resources are polluted (Composite Water Management Index)
Source of River Pollution:
80% of river pollution -> untreated waste
20% of river pollution -> Industries
Groundwater Crisis
India is the largest groundwater user globally (26% of world).
Annual groundwater extraction: 247.22 BCM in 2025 (245.64 BCM in 2024; 241.34 BCM in 2023), out of 407.75 BCM annual extractable groundwater resources (CGWB Dynamic Ground Water Resource Assessment 2025).
The country's Stage of Ground Water Extraction (SoE) is 60.63% in 2025 (60.47% in 2024; 59.26% in 2023) (CGWB 2025).
India consumes more groundwater than the US and China combined
Annual groundwater recharge -> 448.52 BCM in 2025 (up from 446.90 BCM in 2024 and 432 BCM in 2017) (Ministry of Jal Shakti / CGWB 2025)
Ground Water Table -> 0.3 ↓/Year
<b>Dependency</b>
62% of irrigation needs are met through groundwater.
85% of rural and 50% of urban water supply depend on groundwater.
10.8% of groundwater assessment units (730 out of 6,762 units) are over-exploited in 2025 (down from 17.2% in 2017), while 73.14% (4,946 units) are in the Safe category (up from 62.6% in 2017) (CGWB 2025)
GW depletion rate could triple by 2080, if Indian farmers continued to draw groundwater at the current rate.
