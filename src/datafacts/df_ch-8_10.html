Monetary policy

BANKING & MONEY MARKET

DATA: FY25-FY26 (Sep 2025)
Banking - NPA
Gross NPAs: Multi-decadal low of 2.2% in September 2025 (and Net NPA at record low of 0.5%), down from 2.6% in September 2024 [RBI / Economic Survey 2025-26]
Bad Loans: Banks have written off NPAs worth about Rs 16.35 trillion in last 10 financial years
Credit card NPAs spiked 500% in past four years [RBI data]
Banks
Bank Accounts (PMJDY): increased from 14.72 Cr in March 2015 to 55.02 Cr as of March 2025 (with 36.63 Cr in rural/semi-urban areas and ~56% owned by women) [Economic Survey 2025-26].
Credit-Deposit Ratio (RBI’s Financial Stability Report):
CD ratio has been rising since September 2021 and peaked at 78.8% in December 2023.
Over 75% of banks with CD ratios > 75% are private sector banks.
Capital-to-risk-weighted assets ratio (CRAR) for SCBs stands at ~16.7–17.2% in FY25/FY26, well above the 11.5% regulatory norm (RBI FSR / Economic Survey 2025-26)
Profits:
Public sector banks record cumulative net profit of Rs 1.78 lakh crore for FY25.
All 12 PSBs reported profits, marking a significant turnaround from losses in FY18.
HDFC Bank remains the most profitable private bank in India, with a net profit of Rs 73,440 crore in FY25.
Regional Rural Banks (RRBs)
92% branches in Rural/Semi-Urban areas
Around 90% of loans goes to Priority sector lending, with around 70% of loans to the Agriculture Sector.
