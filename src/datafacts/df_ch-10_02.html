Temperature Rise
Planet already warmed at least 1.1 degrees Celsius
77% of climate experts expect temperature rise > 2.5° by 2100
Rising sea levels
Risen by over 10 cm between 1993 and 2024 (NASA)
Projection: Global mean SLR of 1.3-1.6 m by 2100 in high-emission scenario (IPCC).

Greenhouse Gas Emissions [UNEP Emissions Gap Report 2024 & 2025]
Ranking: India ranks 3rd in total GHG emissions (4,140 MtCO2e) [China (1st) & US (2nd)].
Meeting climate ambition: To limit global warming to 1.5°C (UNEP Emissions Gap Report 2025 'Off Target' puts cuts needed from 2019 levels at 55% by 2035 for 1.5°C and 35% by 2035 for 2°C; EGR 2024 cited 42% by 2030 and 57% by 2035):
Nations must cut 42 % off annual GHG emissions by 2030 and 57 % by 2035 in the next NDCs. 5. Warmest year on record: 2024 (NOAA) 6. Degradation and Desertification: 32% of land affected by degradation and 25% by desertification
Impacts of climate change
Ocean Ecosystem
Coral reefs decline 70-90% if warming above 1.5°C [IPCC]
Antarctica losing ice mass at an average rate of about 150 billion tons per year
Greenland losing 270 billion tons per year, adding to sea level rise
Economic losses
Decline in labour productivity of up to 40%.
3.8% of total working hours could be lost by 2030 [World Economic Forum]
Agriculture
↓ kharif maize yields by 18 and 23% in 2050 and 2080 scenarios, respectively.
For every degree of warming, 10-25% increase in crops lost to insect attacks.
Exploitable fish biomass show ↓ >10% by mid-century for many regions of world [FAO]
