Maritime Security challenges

MARITIME AND COASTAL SECURITY

GS3-SEC-0002DATA: 2024-2026
Coastline
Length: 7500 Kms (13 coastal states and UT) → 7th longest in world.
Update: In December 2024, India's official coastline length was revised from 7,516.6 km to 11,098.8 km due to higher-resolution measurement (Survey of India / MHA / MoPSW)
Population along coast: 1/5th of India’s population
Ports: 13 Major Ports (including Vadhavan Port in Maharashtra) and 200+ Non-Major Ports, with major ports' capacity reaching 1,728 MMTPA in 2026 (MoPSW / PIB Aug 2026).
Coastal monitoring and surveillance: 46 radar stations established
SOURCES

Organized Crime and Terrorism

ORGANISED CRIME

GS3-SEC-0006DATA: 2020-2024
Drug Trafficking
Source of Drugs: Drug trafficking through Arabian Sea & Bay of Bengal, estimated to account for around 70% of total illegal drugs smuggled into India
Target: MHA aims to make India drug free by 2047.
Seizure: ₹11,311 crore worth drugs were seized in 19 instances from seaports in the past five years (Ministry of Home Affairs)
Conviction rate: As in 2022, 54 accused were convicted, which almost doubled to 104 in 2023 and increased further to 110 in 2024.
Money Laundering
Estimated Value: 2 to 5% of World GDP (IMF)
Properties attached: ₹1.45 lakh-crore assets attached under PMLA till 2024 (ED data)
AML Basel Index: India ranked 70th out of 141 countries in the Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Basel Index, 2020
Black Money in India
As per IMF estimates, black money in India is 50% of GDP and the World Bank estimates it at 20% of GDP.
India estimates black money in Swiss banks at $1.4 trillion, while Swiss authorities report only $2 billion.
Human Trafficking
Over 10,000 cases of trafficking with 1,031 convictions between 2018–2022 (MHA); in 2024, over 6,000 human trafficking victims were reported across India (NCRB Crime in India 2024)
