President Rule
Since 1950, President's Rule (Article 356) has been imposed 134+ times across 29 States and UTs (including Manipur in Feb 2025) (Ministry of Home Affairs / PRS).
Since 1950, President’s Rule has been imposed at least once a year in 53 out of 74 years.
The 1960s and 1970s saw the most frequent use of President’s Rule.
