Plastic pollution
India
Annual: Generates 9.3 million tonnes of plastic pollution annually.
Daily: 26,000 tonnes of plastic waste – every day -> equivalent of 26,000 small cars!
Per capita plastic waste production (8 kg per year)
Plastic recycling: Just 8%
Single use plastic waste: India ranked 3rd globally; contributes to 5.5 million tonnes per year.
Global
Waste generation: 268 million tonnes in 2022.
Largest consumers: China (20%), US (18%), EU (16%), India (6%) and Japan (4%)
Per capita consumption ->highest in the US at 216 kg per person, Japan (129 kg per person) and EU (87 kg per person).
Air pollution
World Air Quality Report (2024 & 2025 - IQAir)
Rank: India ranked 6th most polluted country (out of 143) in the 2025 World Air Quality Report [was 5th in 2024 and 3rd in 2023] (IQAir 2026)
PM2.5 Reduction: National average PM2.5 concentration declined further to 48.9 µg/m³ in 2025, down from 50.6 µg/m³ in 2024 and 54.4 µg/m³ in 2023 (IQAir 2026).
WHO safe limit: 5 µg/m³.
