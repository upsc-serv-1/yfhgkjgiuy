Expenditure

Rank: 5th largest military spender globally after the U.S., China, Russia, and Germany (SIPRI 2026).
Total Expenditure: $92.1 billion in 2025 (up 8.9% in real terms from $86.1 billion in 2024; 2.3% of GDP, out of $2.9 trillion global military spending) (SIPRI April 2026).
Union Budget (2026-27): ₹7.85 lakh crore allocated to Ministry of Defence in Union Budget 2026-27 (highest among all Ministries, including ₹2.19 lakh crore capital outlay and ₹29,100 crore for Defence R&D; up from ₹6.81 lakh crore in 2025-26) (MoD / PIB Aug 2026)
Projection: Defence budget to rise five-fold to Rs 31.7 lakh crore by 2047 (CII KPMG Report)
