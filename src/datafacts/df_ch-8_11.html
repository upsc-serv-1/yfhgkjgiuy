NATIONAL INCOME & ACCOUNTING

DATA: FY25-FY26 (up to Oct 2026)
GDP
GDP Estimates (FY25 Provisional & FY26 First Advance Estimates - MoSPI / Economic Survey 2025-26):
Real GDP grew by 7.4% in FY26 (FAE) after 6.5% growth in FY25 (₹187.97 lakh crore) (MoSPI / Economic Survey 2025-26)
Nominal GDP rose by 9.8% to ₹330.68 lakh crore.
Inflation
India's Retail Inflation (CPI): Dropped sharply to a series-low average of 1.7% in FY26 (April–December 2025), down from 4.9% in Apr–Dec 2024 and 5.4% in FY24 (Economic Survey 2025-26).
Projections: IMF has projected an inflation rate of 4.4% in FY25 and 4.1% in FY26 for India.
Food inflation increased from 7.5 % in FY24 to 8.4 % in FY25 (April - December).
Headline Inflation: RBI expects headline inflation to be 4.2% in FY26, if there is normal monsoon and no further external or policy shocks.
Savings Rate
India Gross Savings Rate: 30.7% in Mar 2024
Household Financial Savings to reach 6.5% of Gross national disposable income (GNDI) in FY25 [SBI report]
Fiscal Policy & Government Budget
Debt Profile
Government Debt to GDP (Centre and states) - around 83.1% (by 2024 end)
Central Government [Debt to GDP] – Estimated at 56.1% in RE 2025-26 and 55.6% in BE 2026-27 (down from 57.1% in 2024-25) (Union Budget 2026-27)
Government aims to bring it down to 50 ± 1% by 2030–31
State Government [Debt to GDP] – 26%
Debt of the Central Government:
Internal debt: 94%
Public Debt: 89.9%
External Debt: 6%
External Debt to GDP Ratio: In India, external debt constituted 19.4% of the total GDP at the end of September 2024.
Across currencies, the external debt remained primarily denominated in the US Dollar (53.4 %), followed by the INR (31.2 %), SDR (5 %), and the Euro (3 %).
Foreign Trade
India's Total Exports: Scaled an all-time high of $863.1 billion in FY 2025-26 ($441.8B merchandise + $421.3B services), up from $825.3 billion in FY 2024-25 (MoC&I / Economic Survey 2025-26)
Exports in 2016: It was around USD 435 billion in FY 2016.
India’s target for export is USD 2 Trillion by 2030.
Trade
Deficit: Overall trade deficit -> USD 78.1 billion in FY24 (Eco Survey 2023-24).
Largest Trade Deficit: China [$112.6 billion in FY 2025-26, up from $99.2 billion in FY 2024-25] (MoC&I)
Largest Trading Partner: China surpassed the USA in FY 2025-26 (in FY 2024-25, USA was 1st with $131.84 billion bilateral trade and $40.89B surplus; in FY26, India-US trade surplus was $34.4B) (MoC&I)
