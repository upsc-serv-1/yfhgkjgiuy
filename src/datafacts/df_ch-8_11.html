External Debt to GDP Ratio: In India, external debt constituted 19.4% of the total GDP at the end of September 2024.
Across currencies, the external debt remained primarily denominated in the US Dollar (53.4 %), followed by the INR (31.2 %), SDR (5 %), and the Euro (3 %).
Foreign Trade
India's Total Exports: Scaled an all-time high of $863.1 billion in FY 2025-26 ($441.8B merchandise + $421.3B services), up from $825.3 billion in FY 2024-25 (MoC&I / Economic Survey 2025-26)
Exports in 2016: It was around USD 435 billion in FY 2016.
India’s target for export is USD 2 Trillion by 2030.
Trade
Deficit: Overall trade deficit -> USD 78.1 billion in FY24 (Eco Survey 2023-24).
Largest Trade Deficit: China [$112.6 billion in FY 2025-26, up from $99.2 billion in FY 2024-25] (MoC&I)
Largest Trading Partner: China surpassed the USA in FY 2025-26 (in FY 2024-25, USA was 1st with $131.84 billion bilateral trade and $40.89B surplus; in FY26, India-US trade surplus was $34.4B) (MoC&I)
India’s Top Export Destinations: USA (17.90%) > UAE (8.23%) > Netherlands (5.16%) > China (3.85%) > Singapore (3.33%).
India’s Top Import Source Nations: China > UAE > USA > Russia.
Remittances:
India is the largest recipient of remittances globally
Remittances Received: Record $135.4 billion in FY25 ($129.4 billion in CY 2024) (RBI / Economic Survey 2025-26)
The U.S. accounts for 27.7% of India's inward remittances
Current Account Deficit (CAD)
CAD: Moderated to 0.8% of GDP in H1 FY26 (from 1.3% in H1 FY25; ~1.3% in Q2 FY26) (Economic Survey 2025-26)
Forex Reserves
US $701.4 billion as of 16 January 2026 (Economic Survey 2025-26) and US $734.6 billion as of 2 October 2026 (RBI), providing ~11 months of import cover and 94.4% external debt cover.
Import Cover: It is sufficient to provide an import cover of more than 11 months.
Investment
FDI
Total FDI inflows -> $81.04 billion in FY 2024-25 ($64.7 billion in Apr–Nov 2025; total FDI inflows touched $94.53 billion / equity $58.85 billion in FY 2025-26) (DPIIT / Economic Survey 2025-26).
India's FDI inflows have increased ~20 times from 2000-01 to 2023-24.
Highest FDI Inflow: Computer software and hardware.
FDI Source: Mauritius(25%) > Singapore (23%) > USA (9%)
FDI Top Destinations [FY 2024–25]: Maharashtra (39%) > Karnataka (13%) > Delhi (12%)
FPI
FPI Outflows: FPIs have pulled out a massive ₹1.16 lakh crore from Indian equities so far in 2025.
