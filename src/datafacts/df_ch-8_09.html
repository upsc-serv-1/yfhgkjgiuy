Indian Diaspora
Population: 35.42 million (15.85 million NRIs and 19.57 million PIOs/OCIs) as of May 2024 [MEA Overseas Indian Population Data]
Top overseas populations: Top three countries with largest Indian overseas populations are the United States (5.4 million), the United Arab Emirates (3.6 million), and Malaysia (2.9 million).
Remittance Share: India got 14.3% of global remittances in 2024
Indian-American contributions:
The US is the top source of remittance ($26B out of $113 billion in 2022- 2023).
~20% of Indian unicorns have cofounders who have leveraged US higher education.
13% of US scientific publications had Indian American co-author.
1 in 10 Americans practices yoga (2023)
Remittances
Remittances Inflow: Reached a record USD 135.4 billion in FY 2024-25 (up from USD 118.7 billion in FY 2023-24 and USD 129.4 billion in CY 2024) (RBI / Economic Survey 2025-26).
Top Sources: Top 5 source countries: US (27.7%) followed by the UAE, the UK, Saudi Arabia, and Singapore.
Top Recipients: Top 3 recipient states in India: Maharashtra (20.5%), followed by Kerala and Tamil Nadu.
GDP contribution: Economic contribution: 3-3.5% of India’s GDP since 2000
Kerala: Over 36% of Kerala’s state domestic product, boosting per capita income.

BRICS
GDP: Expanded BRICS+ accounts for ~37.3% of world GDP in PPP terms (compared to EU's 14.5% and G7's 29.3%) (IMF / BRICS 2024-25)

Total Intra-BRICS Trade: US$614.8 billion as of 2022.
Powerful driver for the global economy:
Over the past decade, the combined BRICS GDP has grown by 179%.
Total trade of the member nations has expanded by 94%.
