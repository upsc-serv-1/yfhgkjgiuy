GS2-GOV-0017DATA: 2024-2026
Sports Ecosystem
Olympic 2036: India has submitted a formal Letter of Intent to the IOC to host the 2036 Olympics; Union Budget 2026-27 also announced the Khelo India Mission to transform the sports sector over the next decade (Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports / Union Budget 2026-27)
Sports participation: Only around 6% of India’s population participates in sports (20% in countries like the US, Australia and as high as 60% in Japan)
Paris Olympics 2024: India sent 117 athletes (winning 6 medals: 1 silver, 5 bronze; alongside a record 29 medals at the Paris Paralympics 2024) [594 athletes from the U.S., 572 from France, and 460 from Australia] (IOA / MYAS).
Aspiration: top ten sports nation by 2036 and a top 5 by 2047.
Khelo India scheme spent only Rs 318 crore out of Rs 500 crore allocated in 2019-20 (Parliamentary Standing Committee on Human Resource Development).
