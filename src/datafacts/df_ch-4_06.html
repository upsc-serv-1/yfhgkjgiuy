South Korea
Trade Relations: $27.8 billion in FY 2022-23 (India's merchandise exports to South Korea under CEPA stood at $6.01 billion ($6,012.03 million) in FY 2025-26) (MoC&I / PIB July 2026).
Trade Target: India and South Korea aim to reach a bilateral trade volume of USD 50 billion by 2030
Exports and Imports: India's imports were $21.13 billion, while exports were only $6.41 billion.
Japan
Bilateral Trade: Stood at $22.85 billion in FY24 (India's merchandise exports to Japan under CEPA reached $6.04 billion ($6,039.50 million) in FY 2025-26) (MoC&I / PIB July 2026).
Exports and Imports: Exports from Japan to India during 2023-24 were US$ 17.69 billion and imports were US$ 5.15 billion.
FDI: Japan is among the top 5 FDI sources
Economic Rank: India surpassed Japan to become the 4th largest economy in the world
Agreement: India-Japan CEPA (2011) covers trade, services, investments, and IPRs.
Australia
Bilateral Trade: ~$24.1 billion merchandise trade in FY 2024-25 (~$30 billion total trade; India's merchandise exports to Australia under ECTA stood at $7.28 billion ($7,284.17 million) in FY 2025-26) (MoC&I / PIB July–Aug 2026)
Trade Rank: India is Australia's fifth largest trading partner
Exports and Imports: India's goods exports reaching $7.94 billion and imports at $16.15 billion.
