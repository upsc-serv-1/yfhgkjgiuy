R&D And IPR

DATA: FY 2024-25 to 2026 (GII 2026 & Patents FY26)
Gross expenditure on R&D (GERD)
Gross expenditure on R&D (GERD) ↑ from ₹60,196 crore in FY11 to about ₹1,27,381 crore in FY21.
Central Government (43.7%), State Governments (6.7%), Higher Education (8.8%) & Public Sector Industry (4.4%).
India’s Low GERD as percentage of GDP: 0.64% in 2020–21 [Developed countries >2% of their GDP on R&D].
Low Private Sector Contribution: 36.4% in 2020-21
Women participation: In extramural R&D projects has increased significantly to 25% in 2019–20 from 13% in 2000–01.
Research output
Research output: India is world’s 4th in research output, but ranks 9th in citations
India strong in chemistry research, with the subject making up almost 60% of its total Nature Index output.
India aims to overtake US in the number of scientific publications by 2029
Patent applications
Patent applications: Surged to 1,43,729 patent applications filed in FY 2025-26 (and 1,10,375 in FY 2024-25, up from 58,503 in FY 2020-21; placing India in the global Top 6 for patent filings) (MeitY / CGPDTM / PIB May 2026).
Patent grants
Patent grants: Over 1,03,057 (one lakh+) patents granted in FY24 (up nearly 17-fold from 5,978 in 2014-15) (DPIIT / CGPDTM)
Term of Patent: Every patent granted is 20 years from the date of filing of the application.
Disposal of patent application
Disposal of patent application: In India, it takes about 58 months on average to dispose of a patent application ( 20 months in China and 23 months in US).
Global rank
Global rank (GII): India ranks 38th out of 139 economies in the Global Innovation Index (GII) 2025 and GII 2026 (up from 39th in GII 2024 and 81st in 2015; ranks 1st among 36 lower-middle-income economies and 1st globally in ICT services exports) (WIPO GII 2026)
Global rank (IP Index): India ranked 42nd–43rd out of 55 economies in the International Intellectual Property Index (US Chamber of Commerce GIPC 2024/2025)
Low student enrollment in advanced programs (total enrolment in Ph.D. stood at 2.12 lakh in 2021-22- as per All India Survey on Higher Education).
PhDs Produced: India annually awards 40,813 PhDs, ranking third globally, after the United States and China.
Future Prospects: India to spend at least 1%, but ideally 3%, of its GDP every year until 2047 on R&D for science to have a meaningful impact on development.
