Saudi Arabia
Bilateral Trade: ~USD 43 billion (2023-24).

Qatar
Bilateral Trade: USD 14.08 billion (2023-24)
Target: Aim to double bilateral trade from $14 billion to $28 billion in the next five years.
LNG: Qatar is India's largest LNG supplier, providing over 40% of imports.
Exports and Imports: In 2023-24, the country's imports from Qatar stood at USD 12.34 billion, while its exports were only USD 1.7 billion.
Exports Detail: India's primary exports to Qatar include Basmati rice (USD 124 million), articles of iron and steel (USD 200 million), and machinery (USD 106 million)
Imports Detail: A large portion (85 per cent) of India's imports from Qatar comprises LNG (USD 6.3 billion), butane, propane mostly for LPG production (USD 3.1 billion), and petroleum crude (USD 1.1 billion)
