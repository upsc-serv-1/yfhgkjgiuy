<b>Rooftop solar (RTS)</b>
Market: Surged from 2.5 GW in FY21 to 25.73 GW cumulative rooftop solar capacity as of March 2026 (with 8.71 GW added in FY26 alone) (MNRE 2026)
Capacity addition: India added 1.2 GW of rooftop solar capacity in the first quarter (Q1) of 2025, up 232% year-over-year from 366.5 MW
Households: Over 42 lakh households benefited from rooftop solar installations till March 2026 (with PM Surya Ghar touching 18.71 lakh installations / 22.7 lakh households in FY26, up from 8.51 lakh installations in FY25) (MNRE 2026)
New Solar Power Scheme: Electrifies 100,000 households in tribal areas, providing off-grid solar systems for 63,000 villages, 1,500 multi-purpose centers, and 2,000 public institutions under the Pradhan Mantri Janjati Adivasi Nyaya Maha Abhiyan.
PM-Surya Ghar Yojana: Aims for rooftop solar installations in 10 million households, targeting 40-45 GW capacity by 2027.
