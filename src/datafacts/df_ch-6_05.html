Space economy
India: Current size: $8.4 billion (around 2-3% of global space economy); expected to reach $44 billion by 2033.
Global: $570 billion
Funding: ↓ 55% to $59.1 mn in 2024 (Tracxn data)
ISRO
Annual budget: ~₹13,416 crore (~$1.6 billion in Union Budget 2025-26 / 2026-27, plus ₹1,000 crore Venture Capital Fund for space startups approved under IN-SPACe) (Department of Space)
Low cost: India spent $74m on the Mars orbiter Mangalyaan and $75m on last year’s historic Chandrayaan-3 – less than the $100m spent on the sci-fi thriller Gravity!!
Space docking (SpaDeX, Jan 2025): India became the 4th country (after the US, Russia, and China) to achieve autonomous satellite space docking via ISRO's SpaDeX mission (ISRO / Economic Survey 2025-26).
Private sector in space
Over 250+ space startups are now active in India, leveraging ISRO and IN-SPACe facilities (Department of Space / IN-SPACe)
Total funding raised -> fell 35% YoY to $81 Mn last year (Inc42’s Indian Startup Funding Report 2024)

Travel to the International Space Station (ISS) is estimated to cost between 20 to 25 million dollars.
