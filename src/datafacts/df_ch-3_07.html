RTI

Pending Cases: >4.05 lakh appeals and complaints pending across 29 Information Commissions (Satark Nagrik Sangathan Report 2023-24).

Social Audit

DATA: 2019-2026
Social Audit in MGNREGA
Coverage: In 2019-20, social audits were carried out at least once in 63% of the total GPs (47% in 2018-19) (Ministry of Rural Development / Parliamentary Standing Committee).
Out of the 34 States and UTs only six have completed social audit of works done under MGNREGA in more than 50% of gram panchayats.
Over 5.8+ crore assets have been geo-tagged under MGNREGA GeoMGNREGA (up from 5.17 crore in Dec 2022; Economic Survey 2025-26 also highlights Viksit Bharat-GRAMG statutory overhaul aligning rural employment with Viksit Bharat 2047) (MoRD / Economic Survey 2025-26)
