MULTILATERAL

DATA: 2023-2026
BRICS
GDP: Expanded BRICS+ accounts for ~37.3% of world GDP in PPP terms (compared to EU's 14.5% and G7's 29.3%) (IMF / BRICS 2024-25)
Population: BRICS countries collectively account for 46% of the global population, with significant populations in China and India
Total Intra-BRICS Trade: US$614.8 billion as of 2022.
Powerful driver for the global economy:
Over the past decade, the combined BRICS GDP has grown by 179%.
Total trade of the member nations has expanded by 94%.
SAARC
Geography: 3% of the world's land area
Population: 21% of the world's population
Global GDP: 5.21% (US$4.47 trillion) of the global economy, as of 2021.
Intra SAARC Trade: Their intra-regional trade stands at just 5.8%.
BIMSTEC
Population: Home to around 1.5 billion people that constitute around 22% of the global population
GDP: Combined Gross Domestic Product (GDP) of USD 3.8 trillion.
Intra-BIMSTEC trade: Around 7% of the total trade among member countries as of the latest data.
Trade Volume: The total intra-BIMSTEC trade volume was about $60 billion in 2023
Trade potential: The Bay of Bengal is the largest bay in the world and is the route for about 25 percent of global trade.
G20
Population: Almost two-thirds of the global population
Global GDP: 75% of global trade, and 85% of the world's GDP.
CO2 emissions: The G20 countries are responsible for approximately 80% of global carbon dioxide emissions.
Military spending: The G20 countries account for approximately 85% of global military spending.
Foreign direct investment: The G20 countries account for approximately 80% of global foreign direct investment.
QUAD
Combined GDP: The combined GDP of the QUAD countries is approximately 35.08% of the world GDP.
Combined Population: The combined population of the QUAD countries is approximately 23.78% of the world population.
Combined Defense Spending: The combined defense spending of the QUAD countries is approximately 46.88% of the world defense spending.
IPEF
Total GDP: The combined GDP -> USD 33.99 trillion.
Total Population: The total population of these countries is around 2.451 billion people.
