HOW TO USE THIS BOOK

How a card is built. Title on top. The data window sits next to the title — it tells you which years the numbers belong to. Inside, facts sit in two columns, grouped by theme; key exam numbers are picked out in orange. Sources sit inline with the facts as dotted-underline links.

How to quote in the exam. Give the number, the period, and the source name — one line. Example: India ranked 9th on the Climate Risk Index for 1995–2024 (Germanwatch).

How to move around. Tap any fact-sheet title in the contents. Every subject page also lists its own cards. The floating INDEX button follows you on every page.

Print. A4, portrait, background graphics ON — one sheet prints as one page.

Numbers with a small dotted underline are live source links. They open the original report in your browser.
