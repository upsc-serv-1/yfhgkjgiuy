Air pollution
World Air Quality Report (2024 & 2025 - IQAir)
Rank: India ranked 6th most polluted country (out of 143) in the 2025 World Air Quality Report [was 5th in 2024 and 3rd in 2023] (IQAir 2026)
PM2.5 Reduction: National average PM2.5 concentration declined further to 48.9 µg/m³ in 2025, down from 50.6 µg/m³ in 2024 and 54.4 µg/m³ in 2023 (IQAir 2026).
WHO safe limit: 5 µg/m³.
Delhi & Indian Cities -> New Delhi remained the most polluted capital city in the world for the 8th consecutive year (91.8 µg/m³ in 2024); Loni (UP) was the most polluted city in 2025 at 112.5 µg/m³, and 66 of the world's 100 most polluted cities (and 13 of top 20 in 2024) are in India (IQAir 2025/2026).
13 of the 20 most polluted cities in the world are in India.
Global (2025 Report): Top 5 most polluted countries -> Pakistan (67.3 µg/m³), Bangladesh (66.1 µg/m³), Tajikistan (57.3 µg/m³), Chad (53.6 µg/m³), and DR Congo (50.2 µg/m³) [in 2024: Chad (91.8 µg/m³), Bangladesh (78 µg/m³), Pakistan (73.7 µg/m³), Congo (58.2 µg/m³)] (IQAir 2025/2026).
Mercury pollution
India- 2nd largest mercury emitter -> 144.7 tonnes Hg/year
India imports over 250 metric tonnes of mercury each year
India leaks > 220 tonnes into the environment from known sources (Toxics Link, a NGO)
> 70% to 80% of India's mercury emissions due to coal usage.

Noise pollution
<b>Optimum noise level (WHO)</b>
45 dB during the day and 35 dB at night.
Anything above 80 dB is hazardous.
12,000 premature deaths and 48,000 new cases of ischaemic heart disease every year (European Environment agency)
Noise level of >80 decibels for more than 8 hours a day increases changes in breathing patterns (WHO)
