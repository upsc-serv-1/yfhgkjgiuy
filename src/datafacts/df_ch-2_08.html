Poverty
India – Poverty
Percentage: 21.9% {2011, Tendulkar Report}
Extreme poverty: Under the World Bank's revised International Poverty Line ($3.00/day at 2021 PPP, up from $2.15/day), India's extreme poverty rate stood at 5.3% in 2022-23 (and 23.9% at the lower-middle-income line) [World Bank / Economic Survey 2025-26]
Decrease in Poverty [World Bank]
Extreme Poverty Lift: 171 Million lifted from Extreme Poverty in last decade.
India’s Poverty Rate (at the 3.65 dollars per day line) -> Fell from 61.8 percent in 2011-12 to 28.1 percent in 2022-23.
Rural poverty declined from 69 percent to 32.5 percent
Urban poverty dropped from 43.5 percent to 17.2 percent.
Non-monetary poverty (Multidimensional Poverty Index (MPI)) -> declined from 55.3% in 2005-06 to 11.28% in 2022-23 (NITI Aayog / Economic Survey 2025-26) [UNDP MPI: 53.8% in 2005-06 to 16.4% in 2019-21].
