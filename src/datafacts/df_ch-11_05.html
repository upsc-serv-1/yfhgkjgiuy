Left-Wing Extremism

LEFT-WING EXTREMISM (MHA DATA)

GS3-SEC-0005DATA: July 2026 (MHA Rajya Sabha Data)
Decline in violence and deaths
Violence: Maoist-perpetrated violence fell from peak levels to 375 incidents in 2024, 234 in 2025, and 33 in 2026 (till 21 July 2026) (>81% reduction) (MHA July 2026).
Deaths (civilians + security forces): reduced by >90% from 1,005 deaths in 2010 to 151 in 2024 (132 civilians + 19 SFs), 100 in 2025 (64 civilians + 36 SFs), and 11 in 2026 (till 21 July 2026) (MHA July 2026).
Decline in geographical area
reduced from 126 districts in 2013 to 90 in April 2018, 70 in July 2021, 38 in April 2024, 08 in December 2025, and “zero” LWE-affected districts in March/April 2026 (with 37 'Legacy & Thrust' districts and 1 'District of Concern' under continued support) (MHA July 2026).
Security and Developmental measures
Fortified Police stations: 663 Fortified Police Stations (FPS) constructed across erstwhile LWE-affected States as of July 2026 (up from 400) (MHA July 2026).
Telecom connectivity: 9,497 mobile towers commissioned in erstwhile LWE-affected areas as of July 2026 (up from 7,768; plus 15,189 km of roads constructed) (MHA July 2026).
Financial inclusion: 1,804 bank branches, 1,321 ATMs, and 6,025 post offices opened in areas freed from LWE as of July 2026 (up from 1,007 bank branches, 937 ATMs, and 5,731 post offices) (MHA July 2026).
Skill development & Education: 47 ITIs, 49 SDCs, and 179 Eklavya Model Residential Schools (EMRS) made functional in erstwhile LWE-affected areas (MHA July 2026).
Surrendered cadres
1,045 surrendered in 2024
