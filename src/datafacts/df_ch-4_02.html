Neighbourhood

INDIA'S NEIGHBOURHOOD

DATA: FY 2023-24 to FY 2025-26
Nepal
Bilateral Trade: $8.66 billion in FY 2022-23
India's merchandise exports to Nepal stood at $7.45 billion ($7,447.11 million) in FY 2025-26 (MoC&I / PIB July 2026)
Trade Relations:
India is Nepal’s largest trade partner -> 64.1% of Nepal’s total trade
Bilateral Free trade agreement (2009): between India and Nepal
Hydropower: Investments from Indian companies, around USD 2.48 billion. In 2023, India approved the first Nepal-Bangladesh power trade via its territory for up to 40 MW.
MOUs: India, Nepal ink 10 MoUs worth Nepalese Rs.625 million for community development projects (April 2025)
Line of Credit: Line of credit of $680 million by India to fund three major transmission corridors in Nepal - Bheri Corridor, Nijgadh-Inaruwa Corridor and Gandak-Nepalgunj Corridor.
Bhutan
Bilateral trade: $1.34 billion in FY 2022-23
India's merchandise exports to Bhutan reached $1.25 billion ($1,252.86 million) in FY 2025-26 (MoC&I / PIB July 2026)
Trading Partner: India is the largest trading partner (Bhutan imports 88% from India)
Development assistance: India doubled assistance to ₹10,000 crore for Bhutan's 13th Five-Year Plan (2024–2029), up from ₹4,500–5,000 crore in the 12th Plan (MEA)
Foreign direct investments: India contributes to 50% of the Bhutan's total foreign direct investment (FDI)
Pakistan
Bilateral Trade: From 2018 to 2024, bilateral trade fell from $2.41bn to $1.2bn.
Trade Relations:
India withdrew MFN status (after Pulwama)
Pakistan suspended Bilateral Trade (After the August 370 decision)
India consists of 3% of Pakistan’s total trade.
Pakistani exports to India plummeted from $547.5m in 2019 to just $480,000 in 2024. India Exports to Pakistan was US$1.18 Billion during 2024
Myanmar
Bilateral Trade: $1.33 billion in FY23-24
Development assistance: India has already extended $2 billion in soft loans
Indian companies: Essar, GAIL, and ONGC Videsh Ltd. have invested in Myanmar’s energy sector.
Exports: India Exports: US$609.18 million during 2024
Imports: Imports from Myanmar: US$1.52 Billion during 2024
Sri Lanka
Bilateral trade: India is Sri Lanka's largest trading partner ($5.5 billion in FY 2023-24; India's merchandise exports to Sri Lanka reached $5.52 billion ($5,516.83 million) in FY 2025-26) (MoC&I / PIB July 2026).
Trade Partner: India is Sri Lanka’s largest trade partner
