DATA: 2024-2025
Criminalisation of Politics (ADR report 2025)
Member of Parliament: 251 (46%) of the 543 elected MPs in 2024, have criminal cases against them.
171 (31%) face serious criminal charges including rape, murder, kidnapping etc.
Member of Legislative Assembly
Declaration: 45% (1,861 MLAs) have declared criminal cases
Criminal charges: 29% (1,205 MLAs) face serious criminal charges, including murder, attempted murder and kidnapping
Henious charges: 54 MLAs face murder charges, 226 face attempted murder charges, and 127 face crimes against women, including 13 charged with rape.
Gender-wise: 23% of women MLAs (out of 282 analyzed) have declared criminal cases.
State-wise: Andhra Pradesh leads with 79% MLAs having criminal cases, followed by Kerala and Telangana at 69% each.
