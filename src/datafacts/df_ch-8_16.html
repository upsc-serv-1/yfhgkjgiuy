Railway Safety:
200 major railway accidents in the past five years, 351 people killed.
75% of derailments due to `railway staff failure'
10 % derailments are caused by `equipment failures'.
Passengers: 715 crore passengers travelled by the Indian Railways between April 1, 2024 and March 31, 2025.
Net zero targets: Indian Railways targets net zero emissions with 30 GW of renewable energy by 2029-30.
Vande Bharat trains: 162 Vande Bharat train services (plus 2 Vande Bharat Sleeper and 72 Amrit Bharat services) operational as of July 2026 (up from 136) (Ministry of Railways / PIB Aug 2026).
Kavach: Automated Train Protection system (Kavach Version 4.0) commissioned on 2,633 route km as of July 2026 (₹1,547 crore invested till Nov 2024) (Ministry of Railways / PIB Aug 2026).
Airways
Market size: 3rd largest in the world, behind the US and China. (Trade Promotion Council of India)
Domestic Passenger traffic: 165.7 million
8th biggest passenger traffic.
Airport's cargo handling capacity: 8.0 million MT in FY24 (Eco survey)
Airline capacity: doubled from 7.9 million seats in April 2014 to 15.5 million in April 2024.
Operational Airports: 165 operational airports as of July 2026 (164 in 2025, up from 74 in 2014) (MoCA / PIB Aug 2026).
CAGR: 14.5% in no. of domestic passengers during FY14 and FY20
Profit making Airports: Only 22 out of 148 operational airports making profit (Parliamentary committee). The Kolkata airport was the most profitable, followed by the Chennai airport.
UDAN scheme: 679 routes connecting 95 airports, heliports, and water aerodromes operationalised, benefiting >1.68 crore passengers as of July 2026 (Modified UDAN approved in March 2026 with ₹28,840 crore outlay for FY27–FY36) (MoCA / PIB Aug 2026).
Women pilots: 15% women pilots (almost 3 times the global average)
