Road
2nd largest road network in the world (after the USA).
87% of passengers and 60% of freight traffic annually.
Road density: 1,926.02 (2018-19)
Length of various categories of roads:
National Highways: 1,46,572 km as of March 2026 (up from 91,287 km in FY14 and 1,46,145 km in 2024; total road network 63.73 lakh km) (MoRTH / PIB Aug 2026)
State Highways: 1,86,908 km;
Other Roads: 59,02,539 km
National Highway: 2% of total road network, 40% of total traffic
5853 km of NH was constructed in FY25..
Expansion: 65, 569 km in 2004 to 1,46,145 kms in 2024.
Road Accidents: 172000 lives in 2023 - averaging 474 deaths daily, or one every three minutes.
India loses 3% of GDP in road accidents annually (UN Report)
FDI: 100% FDI in roads and highways is allowed under automatic route.
Bharatmala Pariyojana -> aims to develop 34,800 km of National Highways.
National High-Speed Corridors (HSCs): Length of HSCs expanded from 93 km in 2014 to 2,474 km in 2024.
Multi-Modal Logistics Parks (MMLP): Till December 2024, Six MMLPs in Chennai, Indore, Nagpur, Jalna, Jogighopa and Bangalore have been awarded.
Railway
4th largest network in the world - 69,439 route km as of March 2025 (with 99.6% rail electrification achieved by July 2026) (Economic Survey 2025-26 / PIB Aug 2026)
Freight:
In Freight traffic: 12 times more efficient than roadways
Target freight share: From current 35% to 45% by 2030 (India’s NDCs).
Average Freight Speed: 23.6km/h (*USA: 50 km/h)
Over Utilisation: 150% above capacity
Railway Penetration: 0.057 km per 1000 persons (USA 0.752 per 1000 persons)
Railway Safety:
200 major railway accidents in the past five years, 351 people killed.
75% of derailments due to `railway staff failure'
10 % derailments are caused by `equipment failures'.
Passengers: 715 crore passengers travelled by the Indian Railways between April 1, 2024 and March 31, 2025.
Net zero targets: Indian Railways targets net zero emissions with 30 GW of renewable energy by 2029-30.
