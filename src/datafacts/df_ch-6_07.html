Startup
Definition: An entity, HQ in India, opened less than 10 years ago, and annual turnover < ₹100 crore.
Recognized startups: DPIIT-recognized startups increased from ~500 (2016) to over 2.23 lakh as of 31 March 2026 (up from 1.59 lakh in early 2025) (DPIIT / PIB April 2026).
Positive Ecosystem: 3rd largest startup ecosystem in India
High Failure Rate: 90% fail within 5 years.
Employment Generation: Recognized startups have created over 23.36 lakh direct jobs as of 31 March 2026 (up from 16.6 lakh in Oct 2024) (DPIIT / PIB April 2026).
Women Entrepreneurship: Over 1.07 lakh recognized startups have at least one woman director/partner as of 31 March 2026 (~48% of total recognized startups, up from 73,151) (DPIIT / PIB April 2026).
