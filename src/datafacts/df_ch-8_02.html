Economic Growth

GENERAL STATE OF ECONOMY (ECONOMIC SURVEY 2024-25)

DATA: FY25-FY26 (Economic Survey 2025-26)
Global Growth
IMF Projections -> 3.2% and 3.3% for 2024 and 2025, respectively (IMF WEO / Economic Survey 2024-25).
Over the next 5 years → average 3.2%.
Resilient Domestic Economy
GDP Growth: Real GDP grew by 6.5% in FY25 (6.7% in Q1, 5.4% in Q2 FY25) and is estimated to grow by 7.4% in FY26 (FAE), with FY27 projected at 6.8–7.2% (Economic Survey 2025-26).
GVA Growth: Real Gross Value Added (GVA) is estimated to grow by 7.3% in FY26 (FAE), up from 6.4% in FY25 (Economic Survey 2025-26).
Primary Sector: Agricultural growth rebounded to 3.8% in FY25 (4.4% 5-year average; 3.5% in Q2 FY26) (Economic Survey 2025-26).
Secondary Sector: Industry GVA grew by 7.0% in H1 FY26 (Manufacturing GVA grew 7.72% in Q1 and 9.13% in Q2 FY26), up from 6.2% in FY25 (Economic Survey 2025-26).
Tertiary Sector: Services GVA is estimated to grow by 9.1% in FY26 (9.3% in H1 FY26; 56.4% share in GVA), up from 7.2% in FY25 (Economic Survey 2025-26).

TERTIARY SECTOR

DATA: FY 2025-26 (as on March 2026)
Service Sector
Economic contribution: 56.4% of GVA in FY26 FAE (highest ever; 53.6% of GDP in H1 FY26, ~55% of GDP overall) (Economic Survey 2025-26)
Potential: By 2047, the services sector is expected to contribute 60 per cent of India’s GDP.
30% of the Workforce (Eco survey 24-25)
Growth Rate: Estimated at 9.1% in FY26 (9.3% in H1 FY26), after >6% annual growth over the past decade (Economic Survey 2025-26).
Exports: India ranked 7th worldwide in global services exports
India's share in Global Services Exports: 4.3% in CY 2024 (more than doubled from 2% in 2005; services exports hit record USD 387.6 billion in FY25 and USD 421.3 billion in FY26) (Economic Survey 2025-26 / MoC&I)
IT, Computer services and Business Services Exports: ~70% of India's services exports.
IT Sector
