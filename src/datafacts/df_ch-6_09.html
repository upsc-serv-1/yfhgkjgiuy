Insurance Coverage
Health Insurance: 37% covered under Health Insurance Schemes.
Without Health Insurance: 90 crore Indians lack access to health Insurance [IRDAI data]
Health Insurance Sector
Market size: 6th largest insurance market by 2032 (Swiss Re report)
Density: $91 insurance density (global avg $874)
Insurance Premium: Total insurance premium grew from ₹8.3 lakh crore in FY21 and ₹11.2 lakh crore in FY24 to ₹11.9 lakh crore in FY25 (Economic Survey 2025-26)
Rural Areas Variations
Rural Areas: ≈ 20% of Total Hospital
Shortage of Manpower in Rural Areas [Union Ministry of Health data]
Doctors: 70% shortage of specialist doctors
Primary Health Centres: Shortfall of 3.1% of allopathic doctors (Rural Health Statistics 2021-2022)
Rural Community Health Centres: maximum for surgeons (73 per cent) followed by physicians (69 per cent), paediatricians (68 per cent) and Obs & Gyn specialists (61 per cent)
Disease Burden
Non communicable disease: 2/3rd of total deaths
5.8 million Indians die from NCDs each year
1 in 4 Indians faces the risk of dying from NCD before reaching age of 70.
TB: 26.07 lakh TB cases in 2024, marking highest notified cases ever.
India among 10 countries that make up 60 % of global maternal deaths, still births, new born deaths (UN)
Mortality Rate [Sample Registration System (SRS) Statistical Report 2024 (released May 2026) & 2021-23 Bulletin]
MMR: 88–93 per lakh live births (reduced by 86% since 1990, outpacing the global 48% reduction; 93 in SRS 2019-21) (ORGI SRS / Economic Survey 2025-26)
NMR: 18–19 per 1,000 live births (70% decline during 1990–2023 vs 54% globally) (ORGI SRS / Economic Survey 2025-26)
IMR: Declined to 24 per 1,000 live births in 2024 (down from 25 in 2023, 27 in 2021, and 40 in 2013; lowest in Kerala at 8) (ORGI SRS Statistical Report 2024)
U5MR: Declined to 28 per 1,000 live births in 2024 (down from 29 in 2023 and 31 in 2021; 78% reduction since 1990 vs 61% globally) (ORGI SRS Statistical Report 2024)
