Custodial Violence

DATA: 2017-2022 (NCRB) / 2025 (CSDS SPIR)
NCRB Data
Custodial deaths: >660 police custodial deaths in the country from 2017 to 2022 (MHA / NCRB).
State-wise: Guj(80) has reported highest number followed by Maharashtra.
Conviction Rate: 1,888 custodial deaths in the last 20 years but only 26 policemen convicted.
Lack of Inquiry: in 30% of cases.
Common Cause-Lokniti (CSDS) Study
Tough methods: 55% of police personnel consider it important (20% "very important" and 35% "somewhat important") to use "tough methods" to instill fear among the public (Common Cause-Lokniti CSDS Status of Policing in India Report 2025).
Killings: 22% of police personnel believe that killing “dangerous criminals” is better than a legal trial.
Arrest Procedures: 41% of police personnel say arrest procedures are “always” followed. 24% admit that procedures are “rarely or never” followed.
Third degree methods: 30% of police personnel justify the use of third- degree methods in serious criminal cases.

Grievance Redressal

DATA: 2022-2025 (CPGRAMS) / 2020 (TI Survey)
Centralized Public Grievance Redress and Monitoring System
Resolved over 70 lakh grievances during 2022–24 (and over 26 lakh grievances redressed in 2024-25 alone, reducing average disposal time to <13 days) (DARPG / CPGRAMS)
Redressed over 1.5–2.0 lakh grievances per month (DARPG)
Other
Rate of bribing ≈ 40%
Personal connection for grievance redressal: India has highest rate of people using personal connections to access public services (46 per cent) [Transparency international]
