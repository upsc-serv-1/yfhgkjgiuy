Grievance Redressal

DATA: 2022-2025 (CPGRAMS) / 2020 (TI Survey)
Centralized Public Grievance Redress and Monitoring System
Resolved over 70 lakh grievances during 2022–24 (and over 26 lakh grievances redressed in 2024-25 alone, reducing average disposal time to <13 days) (DARPG / CPGRAMS)
Redressed over 1.5–2.0 lakh grievances per month (DARPG)
Other
Rate of bribing ≈ 40%
Personal connection for grievance redressal: India has highest rate of people using personal connections to access public services (46 per cent) [Transparency international]

Police

DATA: FY 2026-27 (Budget) / 2024-25 (BPR&D / IJR)
Budget
Budget: Only 3% of State and central budgets are allotted for policing.
Vacancies
Vacancies: State police forces had ~21–24% vacancies (~5.3+ lakh vacant posts against sanctioned strength) (BPR&D / India Justice Report 2025)
Women in police = ~11.75–12% nationally (less than 8% at officer level; Ladakh = 28%) (BPR&D / India Justice Report 2025)
Police: Public Ratio = ~152–155 per lakh population (sanctioned ~197/lakh; UN recommended -> 222/lakh) (BPR&D / IJR 2025)
Constabulary Constitutes: ~86% of the state police forces.
Weaponry Shortage
Weaponry Shortage (according to CAG audit report)
Rajasthan: 75% shortage
West Bengal- 71% shortage
Surveillance Vehicle Shortage: 30.5% deficiency according to BPRD.
Union Budget 2026-27 -> allocated ₹2,55,234 crore (₹2.55 lakh crore) to the Ministry of Home Affairs (up 9.44% from ₹2.33 lakh crore in 2025-26), with major funds directed towards central police forces (CAPFs) (Union Budget 2026-27).
Trust: Less than 25% of Indians expressed trust in the police.

Prison Reforms

DATA: 2024-2025 (NCRB PSI 2024 & IJR 2025)
Functional Jails
Functional Jails: 1,333 jails | Sanctioned Capacity of Prisons: 4.53 lakh inmates (actual inmate population exceeded 5.11 lakh at the end of 2024, up from 4.25 lakh capacity in 2021) [NCRB Prison Statistics India 2024]
Occupancy Rate [Overcrowding]: 112.7% nationally in 2024 (Delhi highest at 194.6%, Meghalaya 163.5%, J&K 148.3%) [NCRB Prison Statistics India 2024] (>130% in earlier PSI / IJR 2025)
Undertrial: ~73% (72.6%) in 2024 (down from 77% peak in 2021; Delhi and Bihar >87%) (NCRB Prison Statistics India 2024) 76% in [India Justice Report 2025]
