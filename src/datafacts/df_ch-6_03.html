Lifetime prevalence of mental disorders in India is 13.7%.

Mental Health in Students
23% of school children have mental health problems (NIMHANS study)
27.8% of undergraduate students had mentalhealth conditions and 31.3 per cent of postgraduate students had suicidal thoughts.
Self-reporting rate: <1% (IIT Jodhpur study)
Treatment gap: 70-92%
Economic Loss: B/w 2012-2030, is estimated at USD 1.03 trillion (WHO)
Mental health professionals
Only 0.75 psychiatrists, 0.07 psychologists and 0.07 social workers per 1 lakh people.
India short of 27,000 psychiatrists to reach the recommended ratio of 3 per 1 lakh people.
Mental healthcare budget & initiatives: Govt spends ~0.05% of GDP (~1.1% of health budget) on mental health; Union Budget 2026-27 announced setting up NIMHANS-2 in North India and upgrading National Mental Health Institutes in Ranchi and Tezpur as Regional Apex Institutions (Union Budget 2026-27).
