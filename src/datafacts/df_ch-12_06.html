Nuclear Technology

DATA: 2025-2026
Global rank
India ranks 7th in terms of the number of nuclear reactors.
5th largest source of electricity for India
25 operable nuclear energy reactors
Total installed capacity of 8.78–8.88 GW (8,780–8,880 MW) across 25 operable nuclear reactors as of March 2026 (DAE / MNRE 2026)
target of 100 GW nuclear power capacity by 2047
Nuclear power generation capacity doubled from 4,780 MW in 2014 to 8,180 MW in 2024
Union Budget 2025-26 announced ₹20,000 crore for the Nuclear Energy Mission (targeting 5 indigenous Small Modular Reactors (SMRs) by 2033 and 100 GW by 2047); Union Budget 2026-27 extended basic customs duty exemption on imports for Nuclear Power Projects till 2035 (Union Budget 2025-26 & 2026-27).
India's three-stage nuclear power programme
India's three-stage nuclear power programme (by Dr Homi Bhabha in the 1950s):
Stage 1: Natural Uranium Fuelled Pressurized Heavy Water Reactors (PHWRs)
Stage 2: Fast Breeder Reactors (FBRs) Utilizing Plutonium-Based Fuel
Stage 3: Advanced Nuclear Power Systems for Utilization of Thorium
Others
First uranium deposit in India was found in 1951 at Jaduguda, in the Singhbhum Thrust Belt (Jharkhand).
Operational Costing: Estimates show that nuclear plants cost only 33-50% of a coal plant and 20-25% of a gas combined-cycle plant.
