URBANIZATION

GS1-SOC-0005DATA: 2011–2025 (Projections to 2036)
Population
World population: ~57% of global population lives in urban areas (UN World Urbanization Prospects / World Bank)
Population (India Urban Share): 31.16% (37.7 crore) in Census 2011 -> ~36.4% (~52+ crore) in 2024–2025 -> projected to reach ~40% (~60 crore) by 2036 (Census 2011 / World Bank / MoHUA)
Increase of 40% by 2036 – World Bank
GDP contribution = 63%
Increase to 70% by 2036 – World Bank
Growth Rate: India urban population for 2022 was 50.8 Cr, a 2.05% increase from 2021 (World Bank).
Slums
Census 2011 - Total Population in Slums: 6.55 cr Indians live in Slums (around 5%)
Maharashtra (1.18 cr) > Andhra Pradesh (1.02 cr)
Cities with >10 Lakh Population: over 29% population living in slums (EPW).
Bombay (over 34%, Calcutta 32%, Madras 32% and Delhi 31%).
65% of Indian cities have adjoining slums.
Homeless
Census 2011 - 0.15% of population (17-18 lac)
Commissioners of SC - 1% of the urban population is homeless (~37 lakhs).
Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs: approx 23.93 lakh people are homeless.

Total Migration Rate = 28.9% (Migration in India report, 2020-21)

Urban Areas - 34.9%

Males: 10.7% - (5.9% in rural and 22.5% in urban areas)
