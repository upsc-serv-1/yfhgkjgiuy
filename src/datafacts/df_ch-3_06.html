Prison Reforms

DATA: 2024-2025 (NCRB PSI 2024 & IJR 2025)
Functional Jails
Functional Jails: 1,333 jails | Sanctioned Capacity of Prisons: 4.53 lakh inmates (actual inmate population exceeded 5.11 lakh at the end of 2024, up from 4.25 lakh capacity in 2021) [NCRB Prison Statistics India 2024]
Occupancy Rate [Overcrowding]: 112.7% nationally in 2024 (Delhi highest at 194.6%, Meghalaya 163.5%, J&K 148.3%) [NCRB Prison Statistics India 2024] (>130% in earlier PSI / IJR 2025)
Undertrial: ~73% (72.6%) in 2024 (down from 77% peak in 2021; Delhi and Bihar >87%) (NCRB Prison Statistics India 2024) 76% in [India Justice Report 2025]
Funding for Prisons: avg daily spend per inmate is mere Rs 121 (IJR 2025)
Composition of Prisons [NCRB's Prison Statistics India - 2021]
Composition of Prisons [NCRB’s Prison Statistics India – 2021]
Gender-wise: 4.3% are women prisoners.
Vulnerable Sections: Over 85% of prisoners are SC/ST/OBC/Muslims.
Psychologists: Only 25 psychologists for the entire prison population—an average of 1 for every 22,928 prisoners (Recommended: 1:500)
Doctors Availability: 1: 775 Prisoners vs 1:300 (Recommended) [IJR 2025]
Shortage of Staff: ~30–33% overall staff vacancy, with a 46.4% vacancy rate in sanctioned medical staff in 2024 (NCRB PSI 2024 / IJR 2025)
Lack of Child Centers: Nearly 18000 children are living in jails only because their parents are in jails.
