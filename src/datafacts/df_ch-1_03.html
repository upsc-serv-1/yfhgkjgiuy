Heat stress loss in India: 6% of its working hours equivalent to 35 Million fulltime jobs (ILO)
Loss of life: 155,937 heat-related deaths in India in 2021 (Global Burden of Disease study)
Forest Fire
Causes: Only 4% natural causes globally (MoEFCC)
Vulnerability: >35% (~36%) of India's forest cover is prone to frequent forest fires (FSI / ISFR 2023).
Carbon emission: 2.5 billion to 4.0 billion tons annually (IPCC)
Global: Amazon forest – 8000 incidents in 2019
Cyclones
Exposure: India exposed to 10% of world’s tropical cyclones.
Extent: ~75% (5,700 km) of India's 7,516.6 km coastline (revised to 11,098.8 km in Dec 2024) prone to cyclones and tsunamis (NDMA).
Vulnerability: 8% of area and 1/3rd of Indians are vulnerable to cyclone
