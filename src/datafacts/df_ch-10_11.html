<b>Conversion Potential</b>
1700 MW from urban waste (1500 from MSW and 225 MW from sewage) and
about 1300 MW from industrial waste [Ministry of New and Renewable Energy]
Current status: 75% of municipal waste gets collected; only 22 to 28 percent is processed
12 operational and eight non-operational waste-to-energy plants in India in 10 states as of November 2022.
Solid waste management
India generated 170,000 tonnes of municipal solid waste per day
Collection and treatment: About 156,000 tonnes were collected, of which nearly 54 per cent was treated, and
Deposition in landfills: 24% was deposited in landfills (CPCB 2024).
<b>Type of waste</b>
50-55% of waste in Indian cities is biodegradable wet waste;
35% is non-biodegradable wet waste; 10% is inert.
Per capita waste generation -> B/w 0.2 Kg to 0.6 Kg per day in cities with population ranging from 1.0 lakh to 50 lakhs.
Projections: Indian cities will generate approximately 435 million tonnes of solid waste by 2050 (MoHUA 2021).
E-Waste
India
3rd biggest e-waste generator behind China and the U.S.
67% of India's e-waste remains unprocessed (Ministry of Environment, Forests and Climate Change)
Global [Global E-waste Monitor 2024 Report]
Increase in global e-waste generation, rising from 34 billion (bn) kg in 2010 to 62 bn kg in 2022.
Of this 62 bn kg, only 13.8 bn kg is documented as ‘formally collected and recycled in an environmentally sound manner’.
62 million tons of “e-waste” was generated in 2022-> enough to fill tractor-trailers that could be lined up bumper to bumper around globe.
Circular economy
India: India's circular economy could touch $45 billion by 2030.
Global: Transition to a circular economy could generate $4.5 trillion in additional economic output globally by 2030.
