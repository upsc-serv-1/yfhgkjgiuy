HEALTH

DATA: 2024–FY 2026-27 (SRS 2024 & Budget 2026-27)
Health GDP%
Government Spending:
~1.84–1.9% of GDP (Union Budget 2026-27 increased allocation for Ministry of Health & Family Welfare by 10% to ₹1,06,530.42 crore for FY 2026-27) (Economic Survey / Union Budget 2026-27)
World Average = 6%
Target = 2.5% [National Health Policy, 2017]
Economic Survey (2024-25)
Share of government health spending in total health expenditure increased to 48 per cent in 2022 from 29 per cent in 2015.
In FY 2022, India spent ₹9.04 lakh crore on health—3.8% of GDP-> ₹6,602 per person.
Health of Poverty
Out of Pocket Expenditure (OoPE):
Decrease in OoPE: OOPE as a percentage of total health expenditure (THE) declined from 62.6% in 2014-15 to 39.4% in 2021-22 [National Health Accounts (NHA) Estimates]
People Pushed into Poverty: 6 crore people pushed into poverty every year, due to OoPE [NITI Aayog]
