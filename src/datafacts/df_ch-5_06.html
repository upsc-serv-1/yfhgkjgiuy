Transparency Issues - Judges Asset Disclosure
Out of 769 High Court judges, only 95 (12.35%) have publicly disclosed their assets.
Kerala High Court leads with 18% of its judges disclosing assets (41 out of 44), followed closely by Himachal Pradesh at 91.66% (11 out of 12).
Chhattisgarh HC (1/16) and Madras HC (5/65) report some of the lowest disclosures.
As of April 2025, 30 of 33 SC judges have declared their assets, and a Full Court resolution ensures all 33 will publish their declarations soon.

Federalism

FEDERALISM

DATA: FY25–FY27 (15th & 16th FC / State Finances)
Finances
Tax Devolution: 15th Finance Commission (2021-26) recommended 41% vertical share for states in the divisible pool; in Union Budget 2026-27, the Government provided ₹1.4 lakh crore to States as Finance Commission Grants for FY 2026-27 based on 16th Finance Commission recommendations (Union Budget 2026-27).
Borrowing Limit: Central Government enhanced the borrowing limit of state governments from 3% to 5% of their GSDP.
GST compensation cess: might have a ₹1.37 lakh crore shortfall by March 31 due to ₹50,000 crore interest on Covid loans
State Finances (RBI State Finances Report 2024-25 & Economic Survey 2025-26)
Consolidated Gross Fiscal Deficit (GFD) of States: Contained around ~2.8% of GDP during FY22–FY24, and edged up to 3.2% of GDP in FY25 (with State capital outlay maintained around ~2.4–2.6% of GDP supported by SASCI) (RBI State Finances Report / Economic Survey 2025-26).
Revenue Deficit: States maintained the revenue deficit at 0.2% of GDP during 2021-22 to 2023-24.
Expenditure quality improved: Capital outlay, expenditure that leads to creation of physical/financial assets, increased to 2.6% of GDP in 2023-24 from 2.2% in 2022-23.
Language in India

121 languages (22 scheduled languages) spoken by 10,000 or more people in India
