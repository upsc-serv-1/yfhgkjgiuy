Social Sector

DATA: FY 2025-26 / Aug 2026
Social services expenditure (SSE): General government SSE rose to 7.9% of GDP in FY 2025-26 (BE), up from 7.7% in FY25 (RE) and 7.0% in FY24 (SSE as % of total expenditure rose from 23.3% in FY21 to 26.2% in FY25 BE) (Economic Survey 2025-26).
Public Service Delivery
Healthcare
Healthcare Spending: India spent Rs 9.04 lakh crore on health in FY 2022 -> 3.8% of GDP or Rs 6,602/person at current prices (Eco. Survey)
National Health Mission (NHM): 5.23 lakh workers, including 1.38 lakh Community Health Officers. Certification of 7,998 public health facilities under National Quality Assurance Standards (NQAS).
Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission: > 42 crore health records have been linked to ABHA.
Education
Literacy Rate: 74.04% (2011 census)
77% (National Statistical Office (NSO)) -> nearly 8 out of 10 people can read and write.
Mid-Day Meal Scheme: covers about 11.80 crore children studying in 11.20 lakh schools across the country.
Sanitation
Swachh Bharat Mission (SBM): Declared rural India Open Defecation Free (ODF) in October 2019.
Swachh Bharat Mission Phase II – Grameen (SBM-G): During April- Nov 2024, 1.92 lakh villages were incrementally declared ODF+ under model category, taking the total number of ODF+ villages to 3.64 lakh.
Cleanliness Ranking: Indore has distinguished itself by winning the overall cleanest city award for seven consecutive years
Social Welfare
Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT): total DBT was ₹6.91 lakh crore in FY24
Public Distribution System (PDS): Covering around 800 million beneficiaries under the NFSA.
Electricity
Saubhagya Scheme: Achieved 100% village electrification in 2018, and near-universal household electrification.
Renewable energy: total non-fossil fuel installed electricity capacity reached 283.46 GW (including 274.68 GW Renewable Energy: 150.26 GW Solar, 56.09 GW Wind, 51.41 GW Large Hydro) as of 31 March 2026, up from 203.18 GW (MNRE / CEA 2026)
Power consumption rises 7% to 148.48 bn units in March 2025
Solar-plus-storage systems in India can now deliver round-the-clock electricity at under ₹6/kWh, cheaper than coal.
Water Supply
Jal Jeevan Mission
Addition of 75.89% connections since the launch of the mission.
Households with tap water connections increased from 3.23 crore (17%) of rural households in 2019 to 15.91 crore (>81% of rural households) as of August 2026 (up from 15.44 crore in Feb 2025; JJM 2.0 extended to Dec 2028 with ₹8.69 lakh crore outlay) (Ministry of Jal Shakti / PIB Aug 2026).
Urban Development
Smart Cities Mission: As of March 2026, 7,790 projects worth ₹1,56,257 crore (₹1.56 lakh crore) have been completed out of ~8,058 projects (up from 7,479 projects worth ₹1.50 lakh crore in Jan 2025) (MoHUA / PIB Aug 2026)
