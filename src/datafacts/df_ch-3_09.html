Civil Society

NGOs

DATA: 2023-2025
Number of NGOs: Approximately 3.1–3.3 million NGOs (1 NGO per ~400 people) (CBI / Central Statistical Institute submission to Supreme Court; >2.4 lakh registered on NITI Aayog NGO Darpan portal)
Total Donations Received: Rs 55,741.51 crore (foreign contribution) (From FY 2019-20 to 2021-22)
FCRA licenses: ~16,000+ NGOs with active/valid FCRA licences (16,301 as on July 17, 2023) (Ministry of Home Affairs FCRA Dashboard).
Registration and Compliance
Only 2% of NGOs are registered
More than 90% of these registered NGOs do not submit their balance sheets
NGO: People - 1 NGO for 400 people
IB report: Working of few NGOs in the name of protests against the government activities led to a loss of 2-3% of the country’s GDP.

Government Schemes and Policies

Sports Ecosystem

DATA: 2024-2026
Sports Ecosystem
Olympic 2036: India has submitted a formal Letter of Intent to the IOC to host the 2036 Olympics
Union Budget 2026-27 also announced the Khelo India Mission to transform the sports sector over the next decade (Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports / Union Budget 2026-27)
Sports participation: Only around 6% of India’s population participates in sports (20% in countries like the US, Australia and as high as 60% in Japan)
Paris Olympics 2024: India sent 117 athletes (winning 6 medals: 1 silver, 5 bronze; alongside a record 29 medals at the Paris Paralympics 2024) [594 athletes from the U.S., 572 from France, and 460 from Australia] (IOA / MYAS).
Aspiration: top ten sports nation by 2036 and a top 5 by 2047.
Khelo India scheme spent only Rs 318 crore out of Rs 500 crore allocated in 2019-20 (Parliamentary Standing Committee on Human Resource Development).
