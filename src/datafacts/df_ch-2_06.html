INCLUSIVE GROWTH & DEVELOPMENT

GS3-ECO-0004DATA: FY25-FY26 / 2025-2026
Inclusive Growth
India ranks 62nd out of 74 emerging countries [Inclusive Development Index, WEF].
Employment & Unemployment
Total Workforce: 56.2–56.5 crore employed (aged 15+ years in Q2 FY26; over 31 crore unorganised workers registered on e-Shram portal as of Jan 2026, 54% women) (Economic Survey 2025-26)
Need of Jobs: Indian Economy needs to generate nearly 78.5 lakh jobs annually(non-farm sector) until 2030 to cater need of rising workforce.
Unemployment Rate: 5.1% in monthly PLFS (April 2025); annual usual status (ps+ss) UR stood at 3.2% (female UR at 2.1%) (MoSPI PLFS Annual Report 2025)
Self-Employed Workers: It has grown from 52.2% in 2017-18 to 58.4% in 2023-24.
Govt Jobs: 7% of Total employment
Informal Sector: 90%
Net EPFO Subscriptions: more than doubling from 61 lakh in FY19 to 131 lakhs in FY24.
Gig Economy
Total: Employ over 12 million workers [1.5% of total Workforce]
Projected to reach 23.5 million by 2029–30 [NITI Aayog]
Job creation: Out of 100 New Jobs, 56 are being created in Gig sector.
Education & Skills
Stream wise Ratio:
Humanities (33%), Science (15%), Commerce (14%), English (12%)
Pupil-Teacher Ratio (PTR): Steady at 23:1 for the last five years.
University Density (per 1 lakh eligible students): Highest in Sikkim (10.3), Arunachal Pradesh (5.6), Ladakh (5.2), etc. In Bihar (0.2), UP (0.3), West Bengal & Maharashtra (0.6) the density is below national average.
Government Spending:
Expenditure: 2.9% of GDP
Target under NEP 2020: 6% of GDP
Budgetary allocation: ₹1.39 lakh crore (₹1,39,289 crore) allocated to the Ministry of Education in Union Budget 2026-27 (up 8.27% from ₹1.28 lakh crore in 2025-26) (Union Budget 2026-27).
Inefficient Infrastructure [UDISE+ 2023-24 Report]
Computers: The percentage of schools having computers ↑ from 38.5 % in 2019-20 to 57.2 % in 2023-2024.
Internet: Percentage of schools having internet facility increased from 22.3 % in 2019-20 to 53.9 % in 2023-2024.
Electricity: Of the over 14.71 lakh schools in India, up to 1.52 lakh schools have no functional electricity.
Drinking Water: While 14.47 lakh schools claim to have drinking water facilities, only 14.11 lakh have them functional.
Toilets: Government schools constitute the majority of the 67,000 schools operating without functional toilets.
Differently-Abled Students: Only 33.2% of government schools have disabled-friendly toilets.
Digital Education: 46 % schools have functional computers (Ministry of Education)
Universities and Colleges:
Institutions: Total Higher Education Institutions (HEIs) increased from 51,534 in 2014-15 to 70,018 as of June 2025 (Economic Survey 2025-26) [1,168 universities, 45,473 colleges and 12,002 stand-alone institutions in AISHE 2021-22].
Accreditation: Only 37% of universities are accredited
Growth: In last 10 years, number of universities has increased by 60%, colleges up 21% [Ministry of Education]
Increased Penetration in Rural Areas: 43% Universities and 61.4% colleges are located in Rural Areas.
