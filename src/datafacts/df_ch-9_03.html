Urea:
In India, urea is the most produced, imported, consumed and physically regulated fertilizer of all.
Govt spending: Govt spends ₹1,500 per bag of Urea, making it unsustainably cheap for farmers.
India is the 2nd largest consumer of urea after China.

Cotton
Yield: 435kg/hectare; similar to 2024-25’s yield of 447kg/hectare.
Rainfed crop: 67% of India’s cotton is produced on rain-fed areas and 33% on irrigated lands
Acreage: India ranks 1st in cotton acreage with around 40% of the world area under cotton
Production: India ranks 2nd in cotton production with estimated production of 343.47 lakh bales (5.84 MMT) in 2022-23 i.e. 23.83% of world cotton production.
Productivity: 39th rank in overall cotton yield -> lower than countries like USA, China, Brazil etc.
Consumption: 2nd largest consumer of cotton -> 22.24% of world cotton consumption (2023).
