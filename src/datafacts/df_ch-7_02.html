Disaster Stats

GS3-DM-0002DATA: 2024-2025 (IDMC) & NDMA baseline
Internal displacements due to natural disasters: 5.4 million displacements in India in 2024, the highest in 12 years (IDMC GRID 2025); South Asia recorded over 4.6 million disaster displacements in 2025 out of 29.9 million globally (IDMC GRID 2026)
Droughts
Impact: 40% land area and 70 % of cultivable area prone to droughts
Vulnerability: Affect 40% of population
60% increase in droughts in last 25 years
Earthquakes
[Data by NDMA]
Vulnerability: ~59-60% of landmass is prone to earthquakes (80% of population) (NDMA)
Unsustainable infra: 90% casualties due to collapse of houses & structures
Landslide
12.6% of land area is prone to landslides (NDMA / GSI).
Floods
Area: 12% of land area (over 40 million hectares) prone to floods and river erosion (NDMA)
Vulnerable sections: Children and women, constitute 85% of death.
Economic Losses: average annual loss is estimated to be US$ 7.4 billion
Urban fire
Hotspots: Maha & Guj accounts 30% of India’s fire accident deaths
Reasons: 60% of Fire related deaths occur in Residential buildings.
Shortcomings: 65% shortage of fire stations.
Impact: 4th biggest risk to business continuity (India Risk Survey 2021)
Loss of life: 35 people killed every day due to fire related incidents
Vulnerable section: 60% of those killed are women
