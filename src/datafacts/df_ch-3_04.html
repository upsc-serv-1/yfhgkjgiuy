Corruption

DATA: 2025 (CPI 2025 released Feb 2026)
Corruption Perceptions Index (CPI) 2025: India ranks 91st out of 182 countries (score 39), improving from 96th out of 180 countries in CPI 2024 (Transparency International 2026)
Transparency International Survey (Global Corruption Barometer - Asia)
89% of Indians: Think government corruption is a big problem
71% of Indians: Think corruption levels have increased and/or stayed the same in the previous 12 months
46% of Indians: Think that police and local government officials are corrupt
18% of Indians: Were offered bribes in exchange for votes

Criminal Justice

DATA: 2024-2025
Undertrials in India
Undertrials: ~73% (72.6%) of total prisoners are undertrials in 2024 (NCRB Prison Statistics India 2024) ~75-76% in [India Justice Report 2025]
Out of these about 68% were either illiterate (27%) or school dropouts (41%)
The number of under-trials doubled between 2010 (2.4 lakh) and 2021 (4.3 lakh). [India Justice Report 2022]
Time Spent in Prisons: Nationally, 69.9% of undertrials were confined for up to 1 year, while 2.4% (9,028 individuals) were confined for more than 5 years in 2024 [20.8% for 1-3 yrs, 5.6% for 3-5 yrs, 2.7% for >5 yrs in earlier PSI] (NCRB Prison Statistics India 2024).
Vulnerable section: Near about 55% undertrials are SC, ST and Muslims.
Conviction rate: Nationwide in 2022, 23.3% of the total prisoners were proven guilty and convicted for their crimes
SOURCES
NCRB, Ministry of Home Affairs · Prison Statistics India (PSI) 2024 (2026)GoI
Tata Trusts / Centre for Social Justice et al. · India Justice Report (IJR) 2025 (2025)Private

Custodial Violence

DATA: 2017-2022 (NCRB) / 2025 (CSDS SPIR)
NCRB Data
Custodial deaths: >660 police custodial deaths in the country from 2017 to 2022 (MHA / NCRB).
State-wise: Guj(80) has reported highest number followed by Maharashtra.
Conviction Rate: 1,888 custodial deaths in the last 20 years but only 26 policemen convicted.
Lack of Inquiry: in 30% of cases.
Common Cause-Lokniti (CSDS) Study
Tough methods: 55% of police personnel consider it important (20% "very important" and 35% "somewhat important") to use "tough methods" to instill fear among the public (Common Cause-Lokniti CSDS Status of Policing in India Report 2025).
Killings: 22% of police personnel believe that killing “dangerous criminals” is better than a legal trial.
