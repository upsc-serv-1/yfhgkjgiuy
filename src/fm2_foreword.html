Why this book exists

Mains is not a knowledge test. It is a recall-and-writing test under a strict clock. This book turns previous-year questions into small answer units you can revise fast.

Most aspirants already own thick books. The problem is not material. The problem is shape: a 600-page book cannot be revised in the last thirty days. So we cut each topic into nano cards. Each card answers one examinable idea. Each carries a quotable line, a two-line introduction, ready examples, safe data, and a closing sentence.

The 551 cards in this edition were selected and polished from the full PYQ archive of GS Papers I to IV. We kept only what the exam rewards. Every quote, number, name, and judgment is kept exactly as verified. Nothing you read here is paraphrased theory.

Read a card, close the book, and write one answer paragraph from memory. That one habit is worth more than any new material.

Use the How-to-Use section to pick a reading route. Use the index to jump straight to a subtopic. Then trust the cards, and trust your practice.
