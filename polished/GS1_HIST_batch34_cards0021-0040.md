:::introduction
- Indian music spans a vast range of **classical, devotional, popular genres**, reflecting India's deep, diverse cultural heritage.
- Dance has 4 dimensions (diagram): **story-telling, the devotional/spiritual element, artistic expression, cultural celebration**.
:::

:::data
- **Music:**
  - Narad Muni introduced it.
  - IVC: the 7-holed flute.
  - Vedic: the Sama Veda.
  - Later Vedic: Bharata's Natyashastra.
  - Classification: 1. ritualistic vs narrative, 3. classical vs folk.
  - 3 pillars: Swar, raga, Taal.
  - Carnatic vs Hindustani — the 2 sub-genres of classical music.
  - Folk: no hard and fast rules, diverse themes, dance-centred.
- **Dance:**
  - Nandikeshwara's Abhinaya Darpan — 3 elements:
    - Nritta — dance without expression
    - Natya — drama
    - Nritya — dance with sentiments and emotions
  - Music, mudras, and the guru-shishya parampara play an important role.
  - Classical forms (Sangeet Natak Akademi): Bharatnatyam, Kuchipuri, Kathakali, Mohiniattam, Odissi, Manipuri, Kathak, Sattriya.
  - Folk dances:
    - 1. Rajasthan — Kalbelia, Ghoomar.
    - 2. Gujarat — Garba.
    - 3. Andhra Pradesh — Burakatha.
    - 4. Karnataka — Bhoota Aradhana.
    - 5. Punjab — Bhangra, Gidda.
    - 6. Himachal Pradesh — Charba.
    - 7. Madhya Pradesh — Matki.
    - 8. Sikkim — Singhi Chaam.
    - 9. Assam — Bihu.
    - 10. Goa — Tarangmal.
- **Theatre:**
  - The Sitabena and Jogimara caves — among the world's oldest amphitheatres.
  - Bharat Muni's Natyashastra.
  - Folk theatre: Gujarat — Bhavai; Maharashtra — Tamasha/Lavani; Madhya Pradesh — Maach; Odisha — Daskathia; Assam — Ankianaat; Uttar Pradesh — Nautanki/Ramlila; Andhra Pradesh — Burakatha; Karnataka — Yakshagana; Punjab — Swaang.
:::

:::conclusion
- Today classical music lives on in the **Guru Shishya parampara**, alongside western, fusion and newer forms like Indie Pop. Preserving and mainstreaming classical and folk needs **SPICMACAY, Sangeet Natak Academy**.
- **11K dancers** created a world record performing the **Bihu dance**.
:::

  - Strategos — military governors
  - Chronology — Megasthenes
  - Drama — the use of curtains
  - Sculpture, coins, trade, cities
- **Architecture:**
  - **Rock-cut caves:** Karle, Ajanta, Udaygiri; Khandagiri (OD) — Kharavela.
  - **Stupas:** Amravati, Bharhut, Sanchi (Shungas — the Torana).
- **Sculpture:**
  - **Gandhara (Greco-Buddhist):** the Bamiyan Buddha, Bodhisattvas in different mudras.
  - **Amravati:** narrative art — the Buddha taming the elephant.
  - **Mathura (HBJ):** the Jain Sarvatobhadra, Vima Kadphises, the Sarnath standing Buddha.
- **Literature:**
  - **Kushanas:** Vatsyayana's Kamasutra, Ashvaghosha's Buddhacharitra.
  - **Shungas:** Panini's Ashtadhyayi, Patanjali's Yogasutra.
:::

:::conclusion
- Template: **Greek vs Roman**.
- Greek — strength, muscular, idealistic, marble-based, Gods shown.
- Roman — realism, concrete, ornamentation & decoration, real events & people shown.
:::
