:::

:::data
- Recent dilution of the **Forest Conservation Rules, 2022**.
- **Steps:**
  - Green India
  - Laws — CAMPA 2016
  - NCAVES
  - The NAP to combat desertification
  - NDC — a 2.5-3 billion tonne carbon sink
- **Best Practices:**
  - JFM — the Maldharis of Gir
  - Eco-feminism — 111 trees for every girl child
:::

:::conclusion
- The outdated NFP 1988 should be replaced with one in tune with the **dynamic realities of the climate & development landscape**. It should incorporate the active participation of all stakeholders — indigenous communities and experts included — for sustainable forest management.
- Forests hold a key role in the **Panchamrut goal of net 0 by 2070**.
:::

card_title: "RARE EARTH"
card_type: structured
sections: [introduction, data, conclusion]
flags: []
status: polished
---

:::introduction
A set of **17 metallic elements** with unique magnetic & electrochemical properties and wide-ranging applications.
:::

:::data
- **100% import dependence for 7/12**, with no declared sources.
- **Resource grab — the Chinese control 95%**. The extraction dilemma (envt).
- **Steps:**
  - IREL — given a monopoly
  - The Mines & Minerals Amendment Act — EODB
- Why 'rare' — the tech difficulties of extracting them from low concentrations.
- India has **6% of reserves but produces 1% of global output**.
- **Global distribution:** Vietnam, Brazil, China, Russia, USA, Australia.
:::

:::conclusion
Set up an **Indian Rare Earth Mission** on the lines of the semiconductor mission, to promote exploration & self-sufficiency.
:::

---

:::introduction
- Minerals: naturally occurring material holding a distinct place in modern industries and energy security.
- **PMN:** rounded rock concretions found on the ocean floor, containing valuable metals like **manganese, nickel, copper, and cobalt**.
:::

:::data
- **Belts:**
  - Himalayan — gold, cu, coal, lead
  - The NW Belt (RJ) — non-ferrous: uranium, gypsum, zinc, petroleum, silver, cu
  - Central — marble, bauxite, limestone, gemstones
  - The NE peninsula — 90% of iron, 80% of coal
  - The SW belt — monazite, iron
- **Steps:** pioneer investor status in the Central Indian Ocean — a **380 mn tonne** resource potential (**10% recovered would be enough to meet energy needs for 100 years**).
:::

:::conclusion
- In line with commitments under **SDG 12**, we must seek **responsible extraction, equitable distribution, and sustainable use of minerals**.
- The **Deep Ocean mission**, which is developing tech for deep-sea mining, can be a game changer — but it must proceed responsibly, without damaging deep-sea ecosystems.
:::
