- **Bronzes:** IVC; Jain — Chausa (BH): Adinath with long hair; Akota (Vadodara): all 24 on thrones.
- Shashandevi/yakshini — Chakreshwari — Adinath.
- Dilwara — Buddhist.
- Dhanesar Khera — Mathura style.
- Sultanganj — Sarnath style — foldless drapery.
- Vakatakas — Amravati style — Poophnar (MH).
- Portable — Gupta, Vakataka time.
- Nalanda — Avalokiteshvara, Tara
- Hills — JK, HP: Buddha + Ganesha, Shiva
- Chaturanana Vishnu, Narsimha, Mahishasura Mardini Durga
:::

:::conclusion
The legacy continues at a renewed scale — Statute of Unity, Statute of Equality (Ramanujacharya, Hyd), etc.
:::

:::introduction
- Use the **12MPRO framework** to structure historical analysis:
- **Ideologies**: Moderates, extremists, revolutionaries, socialists, communists, liberals, conservatives.
- **International events**: the World wars, the Great Depression, the French Revolution, the Industrial Revolution.
- **M - Movements**: Swadeshi, NCM, CDM, Quit India.
- **P - Participants**: Women, intelligentsia, workers, peasants, tribals, foreigners, capitalists, Muslims, civil society.
- **R - Rule through**: the Land revenue system (Ryotwari, Mahalwari, Zamindari), Brain Drain, Education, Admin, Press, Acts.
- **O - Opposition to INC**: the Muslim League, Princely states, etc.
:::
