:::detail label="Concept"
- **Resolution framework:** — 4Rs
- Register the issue
- Restrict the official's involvement
- Recruit a 3rd party to oversee the process
- Remove, or voluntary recusal
- **Choose most suitable of the 5 approaches OR** — Steps
- Recognise the potential for conflict
- Keep a clear policy on disclosure
- Disseminate relevant information to all stakeholders
- Keep consequences proportionate in policy
- Have a clear arrangement to deal with non-disclosure
- Conclusion: Sunlight is the best disinfectant

:::quote
“The line between right and wrong is often drawn by self-interest.” – Nani Palkhivala
:::

:::introduction
**Ethical concerns surface when institutional conduct drifts from normative standards of responsibility.** The result: **abuse of power, conflict of interest, and eroding public or stakeholder trust**.
:::

:::examples
- **Banking scandals** (e.g., **PMC, Yes Bank**) bared **regulatory negligence and greed**.
- The **Nirav Modi case** revealed **weak ethical oversight in public banking**.
- Corporate **greenwashing** dresses up image-building as ethical responsibility.
:::

:::conclusion
**Institutional ethics cannot rest on compliance alone** — it needs moral culture and individual accountability under it.
:::
