:::conclusion
When **impartiality and non-partisanship hold the Constitution above convenience**, justice arrives with no fear, favour, or faction.

card_type: structured
sections: [quote, introduction, examples, conclusion]
flags: [format_fixed]
status: polished
---

:::quote
“It is easy to stand with the crowd. It takes courage to stand alone.” – Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam
:::

:::introduction
**Integrity is the unwavering consistency between one's values, words, and actions — even with no **external scrutiny** around.** It is the ethical anchor of public trust and the mark of true character.
:::

:::examples
- **S. Manjunath** and **Satyendra Dubey** gave their lives upholding **professional integrity**.
- Again and again **Ashok Khemka** was transferred — his stand against systemic malpractice was the reason.
- **T.N. Seshan's** election reforms were **fearless**; a **non-negotiable integrity of purpose** drove them.
:::

:::conclusion
**Integrity transforms authority into trust**, so governance is not just lawful but morally credible.
:::
