:::introduction
- The **MoHA** has brought in 3 new Bills:
- IPC → **Bhartiya Nyay Sanhita Bill**.
- CrPC → **Bhartiya Nagrik Suraksha Sanhita Bill**.
- IEA → **Bhartiya Sakshya Bill**.
- The new provisions stand out for being **retributive, victim-centric, tech-enabled and simplified**.
- **IPC new sections**:
  - 1. S.110 (organised crime).
  - 2. S.111 (terrorism).
  - 3. Cyber crimes.
  - 4. **Mob lynching — 7 years.**
  - 5. Police accountability.
- **CrPC changes**:
  - 1. Zero FIR.
  - 2. Videography made mandatory.
  - 3. Timelines — chargesheet (**90 days**) and judgement (**30 days**).
  - 4. Forensics made compulsory.
  - 5. Community service.
- **IEA changes**: admissibility of **electronic/digital records**.
- **Issues**:
  - 1. Hate speech has been removed.
  - 2. Detention time has gone up.
  - 3. Little focus on bail.
  - 4. Terms remain vague.
:::

:::conclusion
**MoHA**: "The spirit of the 3 new laws is not to punish but to give justice to every citizen & prevent further crimes."
:::
