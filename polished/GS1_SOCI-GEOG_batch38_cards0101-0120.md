:::introduction
- The most important & abundant fossil fuel in India (**4th largest reserves**).
- **TPPs — 42.5% of installed utility capacity (about 70% of total power generated)**.
- The **2nd largest importer** despite the 4th largest reserves.
:::

:::data
- **Distribution:**
  - **Tertiary:** Rajasthan, Gujarat, Tamil Nadu, Assam
  - **Gondwana:** the Damodar, Mahanadi, Godavari valleys
  - **World:** China — Shanqxi; the US — Appalachian; Russia; Aust; S Af
- **Issues:**
  - High Ash (**20-30%**)
  - Sulphur (**3-7%**)
  - Techniques — open cast, rathole
- **COP 26** — Phase down.
- **2x more emissions than NG**. Coal India holds an **80% share**.
:::

:::conclusion
- **JETP** for the energy transition of TPPs, along with a shift to cleaner tech like **coal gasification, CBM, CCUS**.
- Steps: the **Natl Coal Gasification Mission — 100 MT 2030**; a Sustainable devel cell in the Coal ministry.
:::

- **Thorium advantages:**
  - Proliferation x easy
  - Safer
  - Cheaper
  - Less waste
:::

:::conclusion

- Given the vast thorium reserves, focused invt in R&D tech for expediting throium-based generators would make India **energy secure as well as leverage export potential**.
