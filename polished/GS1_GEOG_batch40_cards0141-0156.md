:::introduction
Jet streams are **geostrophic, high-velocity winds (400-500 km/hr)** blowing horizontally through the upper troposphere, west to east (**20K to 50K ft.**).
:::

:::data
- **Determinants:**
  - Air masses of varying temperature
  - Pressure gradients
  - The Coriolis force
  - Landmasses (Ex: Tibet)
:::

:::conclusion
In the Indian context — a significant role in the **onset & withdrawal of the monsoon**, underscoring the need for greater understanding.
:::

:::introduction
Proposed by **Alfred Wegner in 1912**, the CDT holds that Earth's landmasses were once joined as a **single supercontinent** and have since drifted apart over geological time.
:::

:::data
- The split began **200 million years ago**.
:::

:::conclusion
- Geographers have combined the CDT, plate tectonics & other theories to explain the world's physical features and geological phenomena.
- These theories, with modern tech advances, help humankind **predict disasters more efficiently** — making lives safer and the economy more resilient.
:::

:::introduction
**Harry Hess's** sea-floor spreading theory held that **new oceanic crust forms at the mid-ocean ridges**, driving the slow movement of tectonic plates.
:::

:::data
- It built on **Arthur Holmes' convection current theory** — heat from radioactive decay inside the earth creates magma that rises, forming convective currents: the force behind sea-floor spreading.
- Evidence: **normal & reversing magnetic polarity**.
:::

(Same as Continental Drift theory).

:::

:::data
- An inverse relation with the Indian monsoon — Ex: since 1871, **6 major droughts** are attributed to it.
- **SEA forest fires**.
- **Coastal flooding in S America**.
- **Low hurricanes in the Atlantic (HP)**.
- **Tornado & hurricane in N America**.
:::

:::conclusion
- El Nino's disruption of the monsoon can **severely jeopardize Indian agriculture & food security** — adaptive strategies and resilient farming practices are urgently needed.
